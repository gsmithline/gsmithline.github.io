Causality and Game Theory: Structural Connections

December 2025

Causality and game theory are two mathematical frameworks for reasoning about the world, but they were built to answer different questions. In this post, I explore the structural bridge between the two, built around Multiagent Influence Diagrams (MAIDs), and explain what it really means to ask a causal question inside a game.
